---

### 4. Multiple Attempt System

The app gives students **3 attempts** per question:

1. **Attempt 1**: Basic encouragement, no hint shown yet
2. **Attempt 2**: Hint appears automatically, stronger guidance
3. **Attempt 3**: Final chance with full explanation if incorrect

Students can also click **"Show Answer"** after 2 attempts if truly stuck.

## Key Features Demonstrated

### 🎓 Coaching Personality
- **Polite yet assertive**: Encourages without being pushy
- **Growth mindset**: Frames mistakes as learning opportunities
- **Adaptive messaging**: Changes based on performance (streaks, struggles)
- **Persistent support**: Doesn't let students give up easily

### 🔄 Learning Through Repetition
- **Multiple attempts**: Up to 3 tries per question
- **Progressive hints**: Activated after mistakes
- **No immediate answers**: Students must work through problems
- **Guided struggle**: Coach provides just enough help

### 📊 Progress Tracking
- **Attempt counting**: Shows how many tries taken
- **Hint tracking**: Records when hints were used
- **Performance metrics**: First-try success rate, total attempts
- **Detailed review**: Every question reviewed with explanations

### 🎨 Beautiful Design
- **Modern gradient UI**: Purple/blue theme
- **Smooth animations**: Feedback slides in, shakes on wrong answer
- **Clear typography**: Easy to read for middle school students
- **Mobile responsive**: Works on phones, tablets, and desktops
